Cloud sync setup (Supabase)

This lets your laptop and phone share the same list. One-time setup, free.

Steps:
1. Go to supabase.com, create a free account and a new project.
2. In your project: open Project Settings → API. Copy the Project URL and the anon public key.
3. In the SQL Editor, run the setup snippet (ask me in chat and I'll give you the exact SQL to paste).
4. Paste the two values below and click Connect. Do this once on each device.

Import books

One book per line, in this format (parts separated by |):
Status | Title | Author | extras

Status = To read / Reading / Read. extras is optional: put fav for favourite, or a progress note like ch 120.
Example:
To read | Wuthering Heights | Emily Brontë
Read | Норвежский лес | Харуки Мураками | fav
Reading | 1984 | George Orwell | ch 120

Fetch posters & details

This fills in poster, year, rating and genre for titles that don't have them yet, picking the right source automatically: AniList for anime & manga, TMDB for movies & TV (with Russian titles), and Open Library for books.

All three are free with no daily limit, so you can fetch your whole library in one go — no more running out of credits. Titles it can't match are left as-is; you can fix those by hand.
